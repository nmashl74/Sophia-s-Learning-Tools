Solution Test

    For grown-ups

    A self-test on 7Eb Solutions (textbook pp.10–11), using the book's bold words. Four short "Learn" pages (A–D) recap the ideas first, each with one check question. Then 10 test questions; reminders are hidden behind a 💡 button. Wrong answers give a hint, never the answer.

    • Learn: A solute, solvent, solution. B soluble/insoluble; ethanol, propanone; oxygen dissolves. C conservation of mass; concentration. D saturated; solubility; physical changes.
    • Q1 solute/solvent/solution. Q2 soluble/insoluble sort. Q3 other solvents. Q4 dissolved oxygen. Q5 mass sums. Q6 concentration diagram. Q7 saturated. Q8 solubility table (made-up "magic crystals"). Q9 physical change sort. Q10 word match.
    • The finish page shows how many of 32 test items she got right first time (the Learn checks don't count). It is not saved.

    The book's own examples weren't photographed in full, so the uses of ethanol (hand gel) and propanone (nail varnish remover) are ours; worth a check against pp.10–11. Questions are our own, not the textbook's. Memory tricks are ours.
