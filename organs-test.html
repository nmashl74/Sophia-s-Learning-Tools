Organs Test

    For grown-ups

    A self-test on 7Ab Organs (textbook pp.8–9), Sophia's current Biology lesson (Sparx F345), using the book's bold words. Four short "Learn" pages (A–D), then 10 test questions with hidden 💡 reminders, then a Book hunt page. Wrong answers give a hint, never the answer.

    • Learn: A organs, function, brain, skin. B windpipe, lungs, diaphragm, heart. C the food path (gullet → stomach → small intestine → large intestine → rectum), kidneys, bladder, liver. D plant organs (leaf, stem, root), photosynthesis, storage organs.
    • Q1 organ jobs. Q2 human vs plant sort. Q3 label the body (lungs, heart, stomach, bladder). Q4 food journey order. Q5 true/false. Q6 Dora the deer's dance show. Q7 Princess Lily's sunflower. Q8 spelling check (uses the Word Builder tricks). Q9 windpipe/trachea, gullet/oesophagus. Q10 word match.
    • Book hunt (pp.8–9): which organ Ancient Egyptians threw away when making mummies (the brain); how wide a giant water lily leaf grows (up to 3 m). Neither is taught on this page. The hunt doesn't count in the score.
    • The finish page shows how many of 36 test items she got right first time (Learn checks and the hunt don't count).
