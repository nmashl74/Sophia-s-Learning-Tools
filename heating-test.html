Heating Test

    For grown-ups

    A self-test on 7Ec: Safety when heating (WS, textbook pp.12–13) and Evaporation (pp.14–15), using the book's bold words. Four short "Learn" pages (A–D) recap the ideas first, each with one check question. Then 10 test questions; reminders are hidden behind a 💡 button. Wrong answers give a hint, never the answer.

    • Learn: A hazard and risk, heating to dryness, lowering risk. B Bunsen burner parts and the three flames. C evaporation. D boiling and boiling point (water 100 °C, ethanol about 78 °C).
    • Q1 hazard vs risk sort. Q2 safe/not safe sort. Q3 label the Bunsen (barrel, air hole, base, gas tube). Q4 which flame. Q5 order the lighting steps. Q6 getting salt from sea water. Q7 what makes evaporation faster. Q8 true/false on evaporating vs boiling. Q9 boiling points table. Q10 word match.
    • The finish page shows how many of 36 test items she got right first time (the Learn checks don't count).

    Worth checking against pp.12–13: the Bunsen part names and the lighting order (close air hole → light splint → gas on → open air hole) follow the usual school method; Mrs Pywell may word them slightly differently. "What makes evaporation faster" (warm, windy, spread out) is general knowledge, not from the photographed bold words. Questions are our own, not the textbook's. Memory tricks are ours.
