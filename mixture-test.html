Mixture Test

    For grown-ups

    A self-test on 7Ea Mixtures and separation (textbook pp.5–9): p.5 intro, Forensic science (STEM) pp.6–7 and Mixtures pp.8–9. It uses the book's bold words. Four short "Learn" pages (A–D) recap the ideas first, each with one check question. Reminders are hidden behind a 💡 button, so she tries first. Wrong answers give a hint, never the answer.

    • Learn: A solids, liquids, gases; pure vs mixture. B solution, suspension, colloid. C sieve, filtration, residue, filtrate. D method, apparatus, results; heating kit.
    • Q1 solids, liquids, gases; dissolve. Q2 pure vs mixture; transparent vs opaque. Q3 soluble vs insoluble. Q4 solution, suspension, colloid. Q5 true or false on mixture types. Q6 story: solution then suspension.
    • Q7 heating apparatus (beaker, gauze, tripod, Bunsen burner). Q8 sieve vs filter paper. Q9 filtering steps; residue; method, apparatus, results. Q10 filtrate; dissolved salt passes through; insoluble; dispersed.
    • The finish page shows how many of 42 test items (the Learn checks don't count) she got right first time. It is not saved; note it down if you want to track progress.

    The plain-words meanings of suspension and colloid are ours; worth a quick check against p.9. Questions are our own, not the textbook's. Memory tricks are ours, not the school's.
