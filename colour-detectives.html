Colour Detectives

    For grown-ups

    Preview of 7Ed "Chromatography" (textbook p.16; next Chemistry lesson after half term). It builds on 7Eb Solutions (p.10: solvent, soluble, dissolve).

    • What chromatography does: separates the dyes in a colour mixture.
    • Why it works: dyes that dissolve better travel further up the paper.
    • Method: pencil start line, water below the line, let it run, dry it.
    • Use: matching inks (forensic science, from 7Ea).

    Key words checked against the bold words on textbook pp.16–17 (31 Oct 2026): chromatography, paper chromatography, concentrated, chromatogram. In the book but not on this page: machine chromatography and its peak graph, colourless substances and UV light, and uses such as testing water, food colourings and drug tests in sport.
