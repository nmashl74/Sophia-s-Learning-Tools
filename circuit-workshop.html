Circuit Workshop

    For grown-ups

    Covers 7Ja Switches and current (textbook pp.22–23), 7Jb Models for circuits (pp.26–27) and 7Jc Series and parallel circuits (pp.28–29). Sophia is on circuits now. Key words are the book's bold words.

    • Page 1: complete circuits and switches. Page 2: ammeters and amperes; current is the same everywhere in one loop.
    • Pages 3–4: series vs parallel. Page 5: our own "dance model" of a circuit. The book uses central heating and coal-train models instead, so its questions are left for her.

    Not covered: voltage, voltmeters and resistance (7Jd, pp.30–31), AND/OR switches and truth tables (p.29), and electrical safety (7Je). The ammeter numbers and stage-light scenarios are our own, not the book's questions.
