History · The Vikings

Raiders or Traders?

    For grown-ups

    Matches Ms Hart's lesson "The Vikings: were they bloodthirsty raiders?" (booklet pp.6–7) and textbook 1.3 The Vikings (pp.12–13).

    • Pages 1–2: who the Vikings were and why they came (booklet p.6). Page 3: sorting evidence into raiders vs traders/settlers (booklet pp.6–7, 10; textbook pp.12–13).
    • Page 4: who wrote the sources. Page 5: Sophia picks her own view, with any answer accepted, then gets a PEA scaffold from booklet p.15. No conclusion is given.

    Left out on purpose: the "Blood Eagle" fact box and the details of King Edmund's death. Small differences between sources: the booklet dates York's capture to 866, the textbook to 867. The rhyme is ours, not the school's.
